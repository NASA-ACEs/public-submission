# v2 (2026-11-15, tag `model-v2`)

One change from v1.1: terrain, fuels and canopy enter as **means over 200 m cells**, in the training table and on
the map, instead of the 30 m pixel at the point (`docs/research/ca-statics.md`, `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md` S2). The map
then feeds the models what they were trained on, and the statewide map can use the `full` models: LANDFIRE is
staged on the `ca` grid. Table: `python -m ml.series --statics ca` on the fuel-lake table.

## v2.1 (tag `model-v2.1`): out-of-time test; fuel age fades in 2 years

- **Tested on 2025–2026**, years after the training period (`docs/research/out-of-time.md`,
  `v2.1/out_of_time_2025_2026.json`): 13,296 WFIGS ignitions, 80 large fires. PR-AUC 0.048 [0.022, 0.099] against
  0.012 for the burning index, ROC-AUC 0.805, 45 % of large fires in the top 10 % of ignitions; the model expects 93
  large fires, 80 occurred.
- **Fuel age**: the modifier of v2 (fade 3 years) held for scars younger than 3.5 years and understated risk in
  older ones in 2025–2026 (10 large fires, 5.0 expected). v2.1 uses a fade of 2 years (β = 5.87): odds multiplied
  by 0.03 one year after a fire, 0.27 after three, 0.62 after five, 0.84 after seven. Unseen year 2016–2024:
  PR-AUC 0.0673, gain of fuel age +0.0037 [0.0014, 0.0069]; inside scars 10 large fires against 9.6 expected.
  In 2025–2026: 10 against 6.0. The trees, the LFMC models and every other number are those of v2.
- Bundle: `smm-20261020-nisar-v2.1`, with the layers `ml.spreadsim` needs to grow a fire from any point.
