# v1 (2026-11-15, tag `model-v1`)

The model described in `docs/MODEL.md`. Trained on the real table on the server; code at the tagged commit.

## What changed from v0

| Area | v0 | v1 |
|---|---|---|
| Large-fire inputs | weather with single-day lags, raw SMAP soil moisture, season, `lfmc_hat` | weather of the day, 12-month dryness z-scores, 30/90-day rain, 30-day VPD, 3-day wind maximum (+ terrain and fuels in `full`) |
| Large-fire model | one LightGBM, 15 leaves | mean of 5 seeds, 7 leaves, 200 rows per leaf, monotone constraints |
| Fuel age | none | odds modifier inside fire perimeters of the last 10 years, β = 3.71 |
| LFMC inputs | weather, soil moisture, lags, season | the same plus 30- and 90-day rain sums |
| NISAR SME2 on the map | two-pass change minus the SMAP change | fine-scale anomaly against the track's own history, SME2's own 9 km mean removed, weighted by its measured cross-track agreement |
| NISAR GCOV | not used | fuel state: extent of the fuel-age effect, woody load of the spread physics, a layer |
| Spread physics | none | Rothermel rate of spread, flame length, intensity, direction; one-hour run vectors |
| Training time (both sets) | over an hour with LightGBM's default threads on this machine | about 5 minutes |

The fire gain is not significant against v0's single run, whose seed is the most favourable of five (the study
in `docs/research/fire-model.md` measured 0.0376–0.0466 across seeds, mean 0.0404). Against v0 as a 5-seed mean
(0.0424) the same model without fuel age gains +0.0137 [0.0017, 0.0306]. What is certain is that v1 is no worse,
stable across seeds and better at LFMC.

`core` (no LANDFIRE): LFMC better (−0.35 [−0.53, −0.16]); fire no difference in PR-AUC (0.0203 → 0.0213), lower
ROC-AUC (0.748 → 0.713). Without terrain and fuel the v0 inputs (lags, season) rank slightly better; a variant
that keeps them is under test (`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`).

## Map: Santa Monica Mountains, 2026-10-20 (`result_smm_20261020.json`)

30 m, 609,840 burnable cells, `full` models, 51 s.

- **SME2 term** on 79 % of cells (25 scenes of one track; 62 % of the fed cells are not flagged "recommended"):
  mean size 0.0044 m³/m³, 0.09 z-score units. It changes the risk rank by 0.003 on average, 0.015 at the 95th
  percentile, and LFMC by 0.07 points. Small by construction.
- **Fuel age**: 67 % of burnable cells lie inside a fire perimeter of the last 10 years. GCOV (47 scenes, two
  tracks) shows no backscatter deficit on 26 % of those cells, which are kept as unburned islands.
- **GCOV fuel state**: median 1.05 outside scars, 0.59 inside. Where it shows regrowth the rate of spread is 0.62
  times the rate for the unmodified fuel model.
- Medians: LFMC 79 % (interval 51–105), rate of spread 1.2 m/min, flame length 0.5 m (daily mean wind).
- Served by the backend (`feat/real-results`) as `smm-20261020-nisar-v1`: metadata, tiles, point, grid, vectors and
  line assessment checked.

## v1.1 (tag `model-v1.1`): GCOV no longer sets the extent of the fuel-age effect

v1 treated cells inside a perimeter without a GCOV backscatter deficit as unburned islands (26 % of scar cells in
the Santa Monica Mountains). A check against the Sentinel-2 NDVI drop after the 2025 fires refuted it
(`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`, G2): those cells burned like their neighbours and lie in valley floors. v1.1 applies fuel
age to every cell inside a perimeter. Models and validation numbers are unchanged; the bundle is
`smm-20261020-v1.1`. In it 69 % of scar cells and 13 % of cells outside scars have a GCOV fuel state below 0.7.
