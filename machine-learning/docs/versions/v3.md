# v3 (2026-11-15, tag `model-v3`)

One change from v2.1: the large-fire model gets **HRRR gusts and maximum 10 m wind** of the day (`gust_max`,
`wind10_max`; 3 km, the larger of the 12 and 00 UTC analyses), next to gridMET's daily mean wind
(`docs/research/wind.md`). gridMET's mean wind carried no information of its own (odds ratio 1.03 [0.93, 1.14]
once v2's prediction is controlled for); HRRR gusts do (1.35 [1.22, 1.49] per standard deviation). The LFMC
models are unchanged.

## Limits of the wind input

- Two analyses a day keep about 77 % of the daily maximum gust (0.70–0.83 on 24 days checked hourly); days rank
  well (r 0.96), places within a day moderately (rank correlation 0.75).
- 11 days of 2016–2017 are missing from the archive.
- fuel-lake's `hrrr-gusts` connector needs two fixes to run on `ca` (patch in `docs/research/wind.md`) and
  `--force-scale`; on the server the data are staged in separate lake roots and merged into the lake view.
