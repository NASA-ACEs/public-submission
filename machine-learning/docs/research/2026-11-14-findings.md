# Findings before the dryness-anomaly branch (2026-11-14, evening)

Exploratory analyses run on the lake before `feat/dryness-anomaly` was started. Scripts and intermediate files are
on the server in `~/spaceapps/ml-work/research/session1/` (not in git: they read server paths). Every number is
out-of-sample unless marked. They are the evidence the model design in `docs/MODEL.md` rests on.

## 1. What predicts live fuel moisture (LFMC)

25,141 shrub samples at 325 points, California, 2016–2026; held-out 50 km blocks; gradient boosting.

| Inputs | R² California | R² Santa Monica Mts (region held out, 740 samples) |
|---|---|---|
| weather + SMAP + season + **species** | 0.64 | 0.78 |
| without SMAP | 0.63 | 0.78 |
| without weather | 0.57 | 0.70 |
| season + species only | 0.54 | 0.65 |
| **without species** | 0.39 | 0.29 |

- Species carries most of the skill. A map does not know the species, so the map's accuracy is the last row.
- SMAP soil moisture adds almost nothing once weather is in the model.
- Without species, the level of LFMC at a place is predicted poorly (R² of site means 0.33–0.40), its change in
  time much better (within-site correlation 0.69). Rasters tested as a substitute for species (terrain, LANDFIRE
  fuels and canopy, GEDI biomass) recover about 8 % of the gap. Sentinel-2 and NISAR at the nine Santa Monica
  sites: changes between −7 % and +2 % RMSE depending on the method, i.e. noise.

## 2. NISAR and LFMC

- Santa Monica Mountains, chamise, 72 samples at 5 sites, leave-one-site-out: LFMC from the SME2 anomaly 60 and 90
  days earlier, RMSE 13.1, R² 0.52; SMAP alone gives the same (13.1, 0.52); a calendar fitted to the same season is
  better (10.1, 0.71). The anomaly was taken against the pixel's whole-year mean.
- The causal version (mean of past scenes only) is worse than no model (R² −0.19, 64 samples).
- Statewide, November 2025 – April 2026, 251 chamise samples at 82 sites: R² 0.03.
- Conclusion: "NISAR predicts LFMC three months ahead" is not supported beyond one mountain range and one season.

## 3. What predicts large fires (74,568 FPA-FOD ignitions 2016–2024, 477 ≥ 400 ha)

Held-out 50 km blocks, gradient boosting unless stated; PR-AUC of a random ranking is 0.0064.

| Inputs | PR-AUC blocks | PR-AUC train ≤ 2021, test 2022–24 |
|---|---|---|
| gridMET burning index | 0.008 | 0.007 |
| weather of the day | 0.017 | 0.015 |
| + weather lags | 0.025 | 0.014 |
| + SMAP | 0.027 | 0.014 |
| + terrain and fuels | 0.050 | 0.036 |
| + LFMC (model without species) | 0.050 | 0.040 |
| weather of the day + terrain and fuels | 0.051 | 0.078 (logistic: 0.105) |

- Terrain and fuels are the largest step (+0.023 [0.013, 0.034]). The simple set generalises best to new years.
- Thresholds: daily mean wind above 6–7 m/s multiplies risk by 2.3, VPD above 2.5 kPa by 2.2; the two multiply.
- LFMC has an effect beyond weather (odds ratio 0.86 per +10 points, [0.77, 0.97]; chamise below 60 %: 1.73
  [1.10, 2.70]) but does not improve the ranking (ΔPR-AUC within noise).
- Raw SMAP soil moisture: no effect (odds ratio 1.08 [0.79, 1.49]).

## 4. Soil-dryness anomaly and large fires

Root-zone soil moisture (SMAP L4) as a z-score against the cell's previous 12 months, at the ignition.

- Share of large fires by quintile, driest to wettest: 1.16 / 0.77 / 0.65 / 0.49 / 0.16 %.
- Odds ratio per unit of z-score: 0.32 with weather controls, 0.49 [0.36, 0.66] adding terrain and fuels, 0.51
  [0.32, 0.79] adding season; June–October 0.31 [0.19, 0.50].
- ΔPR-AUC on held-out blocks: +0.006 [0.003, 0.013] (trees, with 30/60/90-day lags); not retained in the
  ≤ 2021 / 2022–24 split.
- The anomaly against the day-of-year norm has no effect; for surface soil moisture its sign is reversed.
- Surface soil moisture against 12 months: 0.66 [0.49, 0.88] with terrain and fuels, 0.95 after season.

## 5. NISAR SME2 at ignitions, November 2025 – April 2026

676 WFIGS ignitions with known size and an SME2 scene within 12 days before; 19 grew to ≥ 4 ha.

- Raw SME2: rank correlation with size has the wrong sign (+0.13).
- SME2 anomaly (pixel against its own mean): share ≥ 4 ha by tercile, dry to wet, 5.8 / 2.2 / 0.45 %; AUC 0.72
  [0.61, 0.81]; from past scenes only 0.69. Weather of the day: 0.53. SMAP anomalies: 0.71–0.74.
- The same SMAP measure over nine years in the same months gives 0.57, so 0.72 on 19 fires is probably inflated.

## Pattern

Levels do not transfer (between sensors, between places, between species); deviations from a place's own norm do.
