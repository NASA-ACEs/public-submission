## N2: NISAR SME2 and the 2026 fire season (pre-registered 2026-11-15 00:30, before the data were staged)

SME2 for California was staged up to 2026-05-24 when this was written; June–October 2026 was still downloading.
The winter result (S1 §5) rests on 19 fires. This protocol fixes the test for the fire season in advance.

- **Sample.** WFIGS ignitions in California, 2026-06-01 to 2026-10-20, with a known final size and an SME2 scene
  at the ignition pixel within 12 days before discovery.
- **Predictor (primary).** SME2 causal anomaly: the newest scene minus the mean of all earlier scenes of the same
  track at that pixel (at least 6 earlier scenes), divided by their standard deviation.
- **Outcomes.** Final size ≥ 4 ha (primary), ≥ 40 ha and ≥ 400 ha (reported with counts; no claim below 30 events).
- **Tests.** (1) AUC of the predictor alone with a 95 % bootstrap interval, against the same statistic for the
  SMAP root-zone and surface 12-month anomalies at the same ignitions; (2) logistic regression with VPD, ERC,
  100-h fuel moisture, wind and day of season: odds ratio of the SME2 anomaly; (3) **resolution test**: the SME2
  anomaly minus its mean over the surrounding 9 km (the part SMAP cannot see) added to the SMAP anomaly: change
  in AUC, paired bootstrap; (4) burned-against-unburned: for 2026 perimeters ≥ 40 ha, pre-fire SME2 anomaly inside
  the perimeter against a ring 0.4–2 km outside it, per fire, sign test over fires.
- **Decision.** The NISAR term stays in the map with a claim of skill only if (1) excludes 0.5 and (3) or (4) is
  positive with an interval excluding zero. Otherwise it is shown as an experimental layer and said so.
- **Amendment, 2026-11-15 01:15** (SME2 staged up to 2026-07-07; no fire-season scene analysed yet). Study N1
  finished after the protocol was written and changed the term the map uses. Added, without changing the above:
  (5) the model's own term, exactly as `ml.grid.nisar_fine` computes it (anomaly from the track's earlier scenes,
  600 m minus 9 km, passes of 24 days, ±0.03 m³/m³), sampled at the ignition and tested as in (3); (6) fuel age:
  observed against expected large fires for 2026 ignitions inside perimeters of the last 10 years
  (`ml.fuelage`, beta fitted on 2016–2024 only).
